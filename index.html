The opportunity

Real-economy trade needs more capital than banks supply.

Every cargo of copper, grain or fuel has to be financed between origin and destination. Banks have pulled back from much of this market, especially for mid-sized traders and emerging-market routes.

This creates short-dated, asset-backed opportunities that investors have rarely been able to reach directly. CommodityFund opens them up one transaction at a time, with the underwriting, documentation and monitoring handled on the platform.

The Asian Development Bank estimated the global trade finance gap at US$2.5 trillion in 2023 (ADB Trade Finance Gaps, Growth, and Jobs Survey).

How it works

From physical trade to investable transaction.

01

Origination

Our team and partner network source transactions from established commodity operators. Each deal is tied to a specific cargo, contract and trade route.

02

AI underwriting

Our models read the trade documents, screen counterparties, check logistics and price the risk. Credit specialists review every transaction before it is listed.

03

Matching & participation

Transactions are matched to investors whose criteria they fit. You review the term sheet and choose whether to participate and how much.

04

Monitoring to settlement

Shipments, documents and payments are tracked until the buyer pays and the transaction settles. Investors see its status on the platform the whole way through.

Technology

AI where trade finance is slow. People where it matters.

Trade finance still runs on paper, email and manual checks. We use AI to do that work faster and more consistently, so capital reaches good transactions sooner.

Our models support the credit team's decisions. They do not replace them.

  • Document intelligenceExtracts and cross-checks contracts, bills of lading, invoices, inspection certificates and LCs.
  • Counterparty screeningKYC, sanctions and adverse-media checks on every party in the chain.
  • Risk pricingScores each transaction by operator, commodity, route and structure.
  • Investor matchingShows each investor the transactions that fit their mandate and criteria.
  • Live monitoringTracks shipments, documents and payments until the transaction settles.
